About Us

Welcome to MICHISHIRUBE

MICHISHIRUBE (道しるべ) is a multilingual fortune-telling website created to bring moments of joy, reflection, and encouragement to people around the world.

Our website offers a welcoming place where visitors can explore fortune-telling experiences in different languages.

We are beginning with a free Thai-language daily fortune experience and hope to introduce more countries, regions, and experiences in the future.

Our Philosophy

We believe that a small moment of inspiration can brighten someone's day.

Through MICHISHIRUBE, we hope to create a gentle and enjoyable experience that connects people across languages and cultures.

Our fortune-telling content is intended for entertainment and personal reflection. It is not a substitute for professional medical, legal, financial, or other advice.
