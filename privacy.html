1. Introduction

MICHISHIRUBE (道しるべ) respects the privacy of visitors to our website.

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you use our multilingual online fortune-telling services.

2. Information You Provide

You can access our currently available free fortune-telling content without creating an account.

If you contact us by email, we may receive your email address, name (if provided), and the information included in your message.

We use this information to respond to inquiries and manage related communications.

3. Local Storage

Each fortune-telling feature available on this website may be drawn only once per day. If you access the same fortune again on the same day, your original result for that day will be displayed without a new draw. To support this functionality, the website uses your browser's local storage to save information about the fortune type, draw date, and result. This information remains in your browser and is not transmitted to our server for this purpose. If you clear your browser's stored data, use a different browser or device, or disable browser storage, your previous result may not be retained.

4. Cookies and Analytics

We have not integrated Google AdSense, Google Analytics, advertising cookies, or analytics cookies of our own into the website at this time. If we introduce advertising, analytics, or similar services, we will update this policy as appropriate and address applicable legal requirements.

5. How We Use Information

We may use information received through direct communications to:

  • Respond to questions and inquiries.
  • Address technical or service-related issues.
  • Maintain and improve the website.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations.

We host this website using GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request information, including IP addresses, to deliver and secure its services. GitHub states that visitors’ IP addresses are logged and stored for security purposes when they visit GitHub Pages websites. Please refer to the GitHub Privacy Statement for details.
